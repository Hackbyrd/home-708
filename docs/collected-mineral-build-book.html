Whole-house standards & finish boundaries
palette

Warm ivory, mushroom/mineral, quiet natural matte oak and warm walnut are the fixed architectural base. Dark frames and restrained hardware remain. Owned polished nickel/stainless and neutral new furniture are localized furnishing finishes, not a Cocoa & Chrome blue/yellow palette.

accepted Stone Choices

Study A pearl onyx-look porcelain at owners bathing walls; C1 quiet cream quartzite-look porcelain kitchen counters/backsplash/waterfall; Study E pale limestone-look porcelain shared-bath vanity wall/short return. Keep the current fireplace finish because D1/D3 alternatives were not finally chosen.

dry And Wet Separation

Premium washable matte acrylic for general dry walls and appropriate bathroom paint outside wet surfaces. Decorative mineral finishes are restricted to approved dry fields. Wet areas require a complete trade-designed waterproofing assembly; tile, grout and Roman Clay topcoat are not substitutes.

wood And Stairs

Approve one physical neutral oak sample against the second-floor reference. All four bedrooms use wood floors with loose rugs. All stair treads/landings remain exposed oak without runners, mats or rods. Inspect stair/guard condition and maintain safe finished transitions.

joinery

Approve measured elevations, representative veneer/edge samples, appliance and AV cutouts, mounting support, service access, ventilation and accessible LED drivers before fabrication. Use retained serviceable boxes or well-detailed modular carcasses where they achieve the same accepted design.

sample Gate

Review paint, mineral texture, porcelain/grout, oak, walnut and textiles together under actual daylight and the proposed lamps. Photoreal renders cannot approve color, sheen, waterproofing or physical tolerances.

electrical Control Note

The control groups below describe independent switching/dimming intent. They are not branch-circuit schedules, permit documents or assumed electrical capacities. The electrician designs actual loads, wiring, protection, mounting and inspections after equipment is chosen.

sequence

Work across the vacant house by trade for survey, rough electrical/plumbing/HVAC and blocking before closing walls. Finish substrates and waterproofing before final wall finishes, flooring and coordinated joinery. Install tracks/privacy layers after mounting support is prepared; deliver furniture last and commission each room after dark. The move-in roadmap remains the separate timing authority.

procurement

No piece is represented as purchase-ready solely because it appears in a render. Fresh selected finish/fabric, quantity, actual fit, delivery route and ZIP 20878 availability checks are required. Keep rejected alternatives separate; do not double-order or duplicate owned pieces.

roman Clay Manufacturer Reference

For the Portola Roman Clay option, use properly prepared smooth interior drywall, preferably Level 5, and its specified primer/application system. It is not for wet areas, exterior walls, trim, doors or cabinets. The optional protective topcoat adds protection and some sheen; it does not make the finish waterproof. Final brand and physical color remain unselected.

Selected finish

Premium washable matte acrylic in warm ivory; warm ivory low-sheen trim enamel on painted doors, casings and baseboards.

Substrate preparation
