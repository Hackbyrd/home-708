Whole-house standards & finish boundaries
design Intent

Cocoa & Chrome is a polished modern house built around smoky gray-brown oak, reflective silver metals, white upholstery and light warm putty/pearl walls. Blue belongs in artwork and removable textiles; butter-yellow is a smaller removable accent. Plants supply green. Do not introduce Mineral & Walnut’s walnut furniture, golden metal, brown plaster recipe or its approved A/C1/E stone selections.

finish Palette
CC-W1
surface

General dry walls

appearance

Smooth light warm putty/pearl greige, with enough tonal depth to distinguish the white upholstery. No cloudy cream-beige plaster effect.

approach

Premium washable matte acrylic paint. Benjamin Moore Aura Matte is the sample system; Pale Oak OC-20 and Balboa Mist OC-27 are competing large-board color candidates, not final selected colors. Choose one connected-floor neutral after looking beside the actual Brown Oak, polished stainless, white fabric and daylight.

substrate

Inspect and repair existing drywall/plaster, resolve moisture causes, fill and sand defects, use substrate-compatible primer and follow the current paint data sheet for preparation, coats and curing. Skim only the affected visible areas where raking light reveals defects. A smooth finish does not require a decorative mineral-plaster coating.

CC-O1
surface

Dry interior floors and stair treads

appearance

Light-to-medium neutral natural white-oak appearance, matte and visibly lighter than the smoky furniture; restrained grain and knots. Neither gray vinyl nor orange/honey oak.

approach

Assess the existing floor construction, wear layer, moisture and condition before deciding to refinish or replace. stair Rule

CC-J1
surface

New dry cabinetry and build-outs

appearance

Smoky BROWN OAK with gray/charcoal undertones and recognizable open oak grain. Satin-matte protective finish. New floor-standing storage has a continuous recessed/flush plinth; no spindly feet or floating storage.

approach

Use sound existing or standard cabinet boxes where practical, with sample-approved brown-oak veneer/solid edging and varied fronts: calm slab doors, limited thin frames, a few reeded-glass uppers or display doors. CC-S1
surface

Kitchen and wet-room counters; selected focal porcelain

appearance

Quiet pearl-white to light warm gray Carrara-like porcelain or a low-contrast white/gray engineered counter surface. Fine gray veining, low contrast, no gold/rust veining, heavy onyx clouding or tan limestone scheme.

approach

Kitchen counter finish is a practical sample-approved quartz or porcelain surface after comparing fabrication cost and existing-box condition. Wet walls use an actual rated porcelain tile/slab product; Daltile’s marble-look collections are sourcing leads rather than a final named SKU. Consider satin/honed-looking wall surfaces and a suitable matte wet-floor version; never assume a polished wall slab is an appropriate slip-resistant floor.

substrate

Tile/slab installer and fabricator coordinate approved backer, waterproofing where wet, substrate flatness, joint layout, reinforcement, edge details, cutouts and movement joints under the chosen system instructions. Approve a full actual tile/slab and seam drawing.

hierarchy

Kitchen uses the quietest fine gray vein. Owners bath may have slightly broader pearl-gray movement. Shared and smaller baths use calmer economical field tiles. Fireplace remains a restrained pearl-gray surround, not an extravagant bookmatched statement. No automatic transfer of Option 1 studies A, C 1 or E.
