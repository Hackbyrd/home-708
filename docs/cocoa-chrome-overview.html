Design principles & source qualifications
  • Only Option 2 is redesigned; Options 1 and 3 remain separate and unchanged.
  • Every owned unit is assigned once; added furniture is separately labelled.
  • All 45 cameras require newly approved Cocoa & Chrome day and same-source night images.
  • Catalog photographs can show another finish or size; homeowner SKU, configuration and quantities control.
  • Exact physical dimensions, material samples, aggregate quantities, stock and delivery must be verified before purchasing.
  • Bedroom 5 is proposed and unbuilt. Actual terrace exterior stair and fireplace conditions require confirmation.
  • The visible release badge uses the website semver only.
