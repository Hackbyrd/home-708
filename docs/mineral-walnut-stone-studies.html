Design recommendation · Fireplace decision pending

A subtle addition, or keep it soft.

D3 remains my preference if you want stone here: the lower surround adds definition while the TV wall stays quiet. The original is a strong choice if you prefer a continuous mineral finish. A, C1 and E already give the house several material focal points, so this room does not need stone to feel complete.

D1 is the choice for a more prominent architectural feature. Its full-height pattern attracts more attention alongside the TV and sibling artwork. Keep all three on the shortlist until one feels clearly right to you.
