Design principles & source qualifications
  • Owned inventory is allocated once across the home; new items are separate purchases.
  • Every camera has a paired day/night view. Unchanged views retain the approved Mineral & Walnut envelope; visible furniture glimpses through doors and mirrors are updated to this option.
  • Exact selected stock, quantity and delivery to ZIP 20878 must be confirmed before purchasing; dated catalog observations are not reservations.
  • Renders are design studies, not manufacturer CAD or measured construction drawings.
  • The penthouse bedroom remains unbuilt; its views depict proposed construction.
  • No bedroom color accents, stair runners or Option 2 blue/yellow palette. Approved stone-look wet-room materials remain.

Photographed cameras follow the real model home. Alternate angles and rooms without photographs interpret the blueprint. Renders illustrate furniture and material intentions; exact specifications, comfort, installation and clearances require site measurement. Bedroom 5 is unbuilt.

The Mineral & Walnut and Cocoa & Chrome options remain separate.
